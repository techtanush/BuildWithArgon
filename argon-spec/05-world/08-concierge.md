# 08 — The Concierge and Route Cards

Nova, the Concierge, is the main agent: the first character you meet, the one who knows the
whole company at a glance, and the one who sends you to the right person.

## Placement

Plaza, `(0, 0, 11.5)`, facing +Z, in front of the kiosk (see city layout). Yellow interaction ring.
The kiosk canopy carries a small sign "Concierge" (canvas texture).

## Behaviour

- Auto-engages 0.8 s after the world fades in (see world overview).
- Answers via `POST /v1/ventures/{vid}/concierge/ask` (company-wide records + directory search; see
  [`../03-company-kernel/08-grounding-and-citations.md`](../03-company-kernel/08-grounding-and-citations.md) §3).
- When the response has a `route_card`, the HUD shows the **Route card** above the conversation
  panel, and Nova's spoken line ends with a natural pointer (generated by the model, e.g. "Priya at
  the Observatory wrote that research — want me to send you over?").
- Questions Nova should handle well (used in the grounding eval):
  - "What's the company working on right now?"
  - "What's waiting on me?"
  - "Who built the search feature?" → routes to `eng.builder`
  - "Where's the pricing research?" → routes to `mr.research-lead`
  - "How much have we spent?" → answers from ledger; routes to `fp.cfo` for detail
  - "Did we decide to go ahead with the idea?" → answers from the decision artifact; routes to
    `pv.decision-analyst`
  - "Who should I talk to about a bug a user reported?" → routes to `su.triager`

## Route card (`hud/RouteCard.tsx`)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ (portrait 56px)  Ask Priya — Research Lead            │
│                  The Observatory · Market Research    │
│  "Priya wrote the pricing research."   📄 source       │
│  [1  🏁 Race there]   [2  ✨ Take me there]   [Not now]│
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- `1` / click Race → `engine.race(agent_id)` (ends the conversation politely: Nova says nothing
  more; audio stops).
- `2` / click Take me there → `engine.travel(agent_id)`.
- "Not now" dismisses; the card stays available in the HUD's top-right "Last suggestion" pill for
  60 s.
- If the target agent is the Concierge itself or the player is already in that building, the card
  shows only "They're right here" and highlights the NPC ring.

## `?goto=<agent_id>`

From the dashboard ("Find in the world"): after the city loads, skip the Concierge greeting and
run `travel(agent_id)` directly.
