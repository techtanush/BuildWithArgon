# Prior Winners Research (what judges rewarded recently)

Research done 2026-10-25. No published winner lists were found for Gemini-specific Cerebral Valley
hackathons, so the evidence below comes from three Cerebral Valley × Anthropic hackathons. Different
sponsor and judges — treat as a proxy, not a rule.

## Claude Opus 4.8 Build Day (San Francisco, June 2026; 310 participants from 1,500+ applicants; 12 hours)

Source: [Meet the winners of our Claude Opus 4.8 Build Day hackathon](https://claude.com/blog/meet-the-winners-of-our-claude-opus-4-8-build-day-hackathon)

| Place | Project | What it did | Why it stood out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Tekton** | Evidence-based 3D reconstruction of historic architecture (Tang dynasty hall; Notre-Dame's spire); 339 incremental construction states | Every component linked to its source ("evidence chain"); independent verifier sub-agents; ~20 checks; "nothing renders without a cited source". Repo: [tangxiya-star/Tekton](https://github.com/tangxiya-star/Tekton) |
| 2nd | **Sim Francisco** | Synthetic San Francisco population from Census data; poll it for forecasts | Ground-truth numbers: 81.3% predicted vs 83.8% actual (2024 presidential), 70% vs 70.38% (Prop A) |
| 3rd | **Custom Universe** | Phone photo → editable photorealistic 3D object for robotics synthetic data | Specific user (robotics labs), real pipeline on GPUs |

Notes: Tekton didn't finish the whole cathedral — depth on one part won. Team advice: "Map the
whole project before you build any of it."

## Patterns (14 winners)

1. All were built around a **specific domain and a named user**, or a **ground-truth number**.
   None was a horizontal "agent platform".
2. Winners **proved** their claims: evidence chains, verifiers, eval numbers, before/after.
3. **Depth beat breadth** (Tekton's partial cathedral).
4. Creative/game projects won special prizes, not first place.
5. The managed-agents prize went to a domain-specific multi-agent system (ARIA).

## How this spec responds

- Every agent answer cites sources and is verified (Tekton's evidence-chain idea applied to a
  company).
- The product ships eval harnesses with real numbers (engineering loop vs baseline; grounding
  accuracy; world score per iteration).
- A named founder and real venture are chosen at kickoff.
- The Concierge → agent conversation is the deep path; breadth (13 buildings) is generated by
  Argon and validated, not hand-polished.
